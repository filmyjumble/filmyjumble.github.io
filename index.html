      How to play

      Each round is a film title with its letters jumbled, from Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi or English cinema. Pick your languages on the home screen. Tap the gold letters to spell it out. Tap a placed letter to send it back.

      • A clean solve is worth 10 points.
      • Stuck? Reveal the actor (−2), director (−2) or year (−1), or place the next correct letter (−3).
      • Tap 🔀 Shuffle to mix the remaining letters again. It's free.
