Filmy Jumble – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Filmy Jumble is a word game. It does not collect, sell or share any personal information, and it has no accounts, advertising or tracking.

Data on your device

Your scores, stats and settings are saved only in your phone's storage for this app. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Anonymous play counter

When you finish a game, the app adds one to a public "games played" total. The first time you finish a game on a device, it also adds one to a "players" total. Only the "+1" is sent: no name, account, device ID, score or other information about you. The totals are kept by Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a free counting service, which receives your IP address as part of the web request, as any website does.

Online Fire rounds

If you play a Fire round with friends online, the nickname you type, your room code and your live score are stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database) so the other players in your room can see them. You are signed in anonymously: no email, phone number or real name is required. Your nickname and score are deleted from the room when you leave it or close the app. Practice rounds against computer players stay entirely on your device.

Sharing your result

If you tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Copy result", the app hands your score text to the app you choose. Nothing else is sent.

Film information

Film titles and credits come from The Movie Database (TMDB) and are delivered as part of the game. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. The game loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when fonts are downloaded, as with any website.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
