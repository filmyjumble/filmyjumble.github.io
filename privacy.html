Filmy Jumble – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Filmy Jumble is a word game. It does not collect, sell or share any personal information, and it has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

Data on your device

Your scores, stats and settings are saved only in your phone's storage for this app. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Sharing your result

If you tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Copy result", the app hands your score text to the app you choose. Nothing else is sent.

Film information

Film titles and credits come from The Movie Database (TMDB) and are delivered as part of the game. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. The game loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when fonts are downloaded, as with any website.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
